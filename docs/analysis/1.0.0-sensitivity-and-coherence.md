Reading: the K1 goalpost and the channel weights move the headline more than data noise does. Data noise is 5–11 % of the headline variance.

## 2. Statistical coherence across the 27 member states (2025)

Inputs: Eurostat `isoc_eb_ai` (D1 E_AI_TANY, D3 E_AI_TGE3, D4 E_AI_TAR), `isoc_ai_iaiu` (D2 I_IUAI) and `nama_10_an6` (K1). The filters match the method definitions, and every member state is scored with the v1.0 goalposts and weights.

Pearson r, raw values:

|    | D1 | D2 | D3 | D4 | K1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | 1.00 | 0.62 | 0.95 | 0.76 | 0.43 |
| D2 | 0.62 | 1.00 | 0.56 | 0.47 | 0.53 |
| D3 | 0.95 | 0.56 | 1.00 | 0.80 | 0.39 |
| D4 | 0.76 | 0.47 | 0.80 | 1.00 | 0.26 |
| K1 | 0.43 | 0.53 | 0.39 | 0.26 | 1.00 |

- Indicator → deployment pillar r: D1 0.96, D2 0.77, D3 0.95, D4 0.81. D2 and D4 lie inside the JRC COIN band (0.3 < r < 0.95). D3 is at the 0.95 limit and D1 is just above it, so within the deployment pillar D1 (with D3) dominates.
- Indicator → headline r: D1 0.58, D2 0.63, D3 0.54, D4 0.40, **K1 0.98**.
- Pillar → headline r: deployment 0.63, **capital_intensity 0.98**. K1 crosses the COIN dominance threshold (r > 0.95): the headline ranking of member states is the K1 ranking (Spearman 0.98).
- K1 share of the headline (w·s / H) across member states: minimum 52 %, median 75 %, maximum 90 %. For EU27 it is 78 % (0.5 × 34.0 / 21.7) and for Finland 69 % (0.5 × 39.3 / 28.3).
- D1 and D3 are close to redundant (r = 0.95; D3 is a subset of D1 by definition).
- K1 goalpost note: the highest member state in 2025 is Ireland at 13.1 % of GDP (2024: 7.0 %), close to the 15 % ceiling. That is intangible-asset relocation by multinationals, not AI use.
