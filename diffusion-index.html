How to read the score

EU27: {{populations.EU27.headline.display}}/100 ({{range_level}} range {{populations.EU27.headline.p2_5}}–{{populations.EU27.headline.p97_5}}). Finland: {{populations.FI.headline.display}}/100 ({{range_level}} range {{populations.FI.headline.p2_5}}–{{populations.FI.headline.p97_5}}). The range shows our uncertainty. The true value is most likely inside it.

100 is not “good” and 0 is not “bad”. The index measures how widely AI is used, not whether that is desirable. 100 means AI has become dependable everyday infrastructure, like electricity.
