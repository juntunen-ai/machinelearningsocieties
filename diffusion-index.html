Two-Way Ecological Balance Meter & Planetary Boundaries (Pillar 7)

Artificial intelligence affects the environment in two opposite directions. It increases burden when data centres draw electricity, cooling water, and minerals. At the same time, it reduces burden when used to balance power grids, cut farm fertilizer runoff, find water-pipe leaks, or spot illegal deforestation. We measure both directions side by side and never subtract modelled savings from metered physical load.
