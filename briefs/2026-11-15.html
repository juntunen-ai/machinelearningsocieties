15 November 2026 - Machine Learning Societies

The FAA switched on an AI planner and left the safety questions open

Signal 9/10 · Stakes 9/10

The FAA stopped every departure in the United States for four hours on Friday. Three days earlier it had switched on an AI-supported planning system at all 22 en route centres. No published finding links the two events, and Congress's October questions on testing and training are unanswered.

Signal

Observed. The FAA issued a nationwide ground stop at 15:54 Eastern time on 13 November and lifted it at 20:15. It was the first full halt since the notice-to-airmen system failed in January 2023. About 3,500 aircraft were in the air. FlightAware counted more than 7,000 cancellations.

The agency named a communication problem at several Air Route Traffic Control Centres, which direct high-altitude traffic. The Transport Secretary, Sean Duffy, said instability appeared at 15:36 in radar and in communications. Switches and routers, he said, "got overloaded with data" in a network that still runs on analogue telecom.

On 10 November the FAA said it would use SMART, for Strategic Management of Airspace, Routes and Trajectories, in all 22 centres. SMART collects about 200 data streams, including weather, flight paths and controller staffing. Specialists in Washington check the output and send advice to the Command Centre. Local facilities may refuse the advice, and controllers still separate aircraft. Air Space Intelligence, a Boston firm, holds a twelve-year contract worth USD 875 million.

Within hours Air Space Intelligence said the stop was "not based on or a result of the SMART system". Duffy called the tool "completely disconnected" from the failed equipment. The Homeland Security Secretary said the government was "looking at that".

Caveats. The physical account works without a model. Routers on the FAA's telecom backbone took more data than they could carry, and the agency stopped departures because it could not see or speak to aircraft reliably. Wired reported that the likely candidate is the agency's ageing telecom network, now under upgrade to fibre. A three-day gap is not evidence of a link.

What is missing is attribution. No public document says which data flows crossed the overloaded routers. The FAA has not answered the questions that 25 House Democrats, led by April McClain Delaney, sent on 9 October: what the safety evaluation measured, and what training controllers received. On 13 October 23 Democrats asked the Government Accountability Office to audit it. The controllers' union asked for the same. No public record shows that the GAO has accepted.

Assessment. A public authority now runs a learning system inside a planning flow that touches every flight in the country. After the first system-wide failure it could not say on the record whether that system contributed. The regulator said no, the vendor said no, and the cabinet secretary said the government was looking.

The remedy is a condition, not a better model. An appropriations committee can attach an evaluation requirement to modernisation money. An audit can establish what the integration was tested against. A procurement rule can require an attribution log. None of these exists today.

Evidence 9/10.

  • P — 3 points: The FAA statements of 13 and 10 November on the stop and the expansion.
  • N — 5 points: ABC News (3,500 aircraft airborne; detection at 15:36). AVweb (the 9 October letter; the contract). NBC News (7,000 cancellations; the USD 50 billion request). USA Today (Eurocontrol; 42 countries). The Palm Beach Post (the union's objection).
  • L — 1 point: The FAA published at 15:54, and the vendor answered that evening.

The sum is P 3 + N 5 + L 1 = 9/10.

Stakes 9/10.

The next instrument is a supervision rule for AI in safety-critical air traffic functions, travelling with the appropriations that fund the modernisation.

Thesis

The ground stop was an analogue failure. The political change is the machine switched on three days earlier. A public authority put a learning system into the national traffic plan before it answered its legislature's questions on testing and training. The state can move USD 50 billion of modernisation money, but it cannot attribute a failure in its own airspace.

Antithesis

The strongest objection is that this is a coincidence dressed as a governance failure. The routers were analogue and overloaded, and the digital replacement was going in during the same weeks. SMART only advises: a controller may refuse any suggestion. A safety case for a decision-support tool therefore confuses advice with control, and the real constraint is fifty-year-old copper.

Forecast

Horizon: through 31 March 2027. By then the Government Accountability Office will accept, or the Department of Transportation's Inspector General will open, a review whose scope covers the SMART rollout and names the safety risk assessment or the controller's role in testing. Probability: 60 per cent. Resolution date: 31 March 2027. Score YES if an acceptance letter or audit announcement meets both; PARTIAL if the scope is telecom capacity alone.

Market test — pro-business. Instrument: the USD 50 billion facilities and equipment request. Actors: the appropriations committees, the department and the established telecom and radar integrators. Who gains: incumbents, because the remedy is more money through existing contracts.

Political consequences

1-2 years: Instrument: the fiscal 2027 appropriations line for FAA facilities and equipment; actors: the appropriations committees and the Department of Transportation; observable: a committee report that ties the money to a published evaluation of AI decision-support tools.

3-4 years: Instrument: an FAA acquisition order for AI in air traffic functions; actors: the FAA Air Traffic Organisation and its safety office; observable: an order that requires an attribution log before national operations.

What to watch

  • Whether CISA or the FAA names the data flows on the overloaded routers.
  • Whether the GAO answers the Larsen-Carson request of 13 October.
  • Whether the fiscal 2027 report attaches an evaluation condition to the money. The 6 February 2027 forecast on the UK agentic consultation resolves within 90 days.
How the Signal is scored

Every edition prints two numbers, and they are read together. The first measures the quality of the evidence. The second measures how much changes if the evidence holds.

Signal, 0–10: the evidence. The formula is E = 3P + N + L, and the maximum is ten.

  • P — primary source. Three points when the claim rests on the actor's own document, announcement or statement. Zero when it rests on reporting about the actor.
  • N — independent confirmation. One point for each newsletter or newsroom that adds a fact of its own. Repeating a press release adds nothing.
  • L — first publisher. One point when the party at the centre of the story published it first.

Stakes, 1–10: the consequence. 1 is one team's experiment. 3 is one workflow in one organisation. 5 is a workflow adopted across a sector. 7 is a procurement rule or standard that changes. 9 is a statute, a liability rule or a supervision rule. 10 is a change in market structure. The even steps are the cases between them.
