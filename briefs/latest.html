23 November 2026 - Machine Learning Societies

OpenAI proposes a speed limit in a unit it owns

Signal 4/10 · Stakes 7/10

On 22 November OpenAI published the pretraining loss curve of its main models for the first time, and proposed that frontier developers agree a common limit on that measure. The numbers come from a dataset OpenAI does not publish, and no other laboratory has agreed to them. The first numeric line on frontier development would sit in the units of the firm that is ahead.

Signal

Observed. Jakub Pachocki, the chief scientist of OpenAI, published "Perplexity and Intelligence" on 22 November 2026. The post plots log-perplexity for each main GPT since GPT-3.5. Perplexity measures how surprised a model is by the next word of text; a lower value means better prediction. The plot gives about 0.99 for GPT-3.5, about 0.91 for GPT-4, below 0.80 for current models, and about 0.76 for the model that began to produce "groundbreaking results" on open mathematics problems. Pachocki expects further transformative capabilities in the 0.68 to 0.70 window. He writes that he does not know what they will be. He proposes "common limits, based on perplexity and other measurements, that developers are committed to not crossing until our ability to understand and monitor AI behavior achieves a certain concrete milestone". The only example he gives is a successor to chain-of-thought monitors, which read a model's written reasoning. He asks for an industry standard that sets a first limit on development. He sets no number. Sam Altman called the post one of the most important things OpenAI has published, and cited the White House Accord on Super Intelligence of 29 September 2026. That accord is a voluntary pledge by six companies. It contains no limit, no threshold, no deadline and no penalty.

Caveats. Perplexity measures the compression of text, not the performance of a task. OpenAI lists four problems in its own instrument. Laboratories train on different data mixtures, so their numbers are not comparable today. Chain-of-thought reasoning raises capability without lowering perplexity. The floor of the metric is unknown, so equal steps in loss are not equal steps in capability. Engineering adds a fifth. Loss falls only when training compute and data rise, so a limit on loss is a limit on the training budget, not on deployment. The curve is a self-report on a private dataset, and no outside party has checked it.

Assessment. For a reader outside AI, the change is that a capability threshold now has a number and an owner. The thresholds in force today are legal and financial. The EU AI Act presumes systemic risk above 10^25 FLOP of training compute. The White House accord asks for audits and sets no stop line. Two things must happen before this changes a decision. A second laboratory must publish a comparable figure. An institution that buys, licenses or supervises models must adopt the figure in a document it signs. The instrument at issue is an industry standard for frontier training. Six companies have already accepted a framework for such standards, so stakes are 7 of 10.

Evidence 4/10.

  • P. One primary source: the OpenAI post and its chart, 22 November 2026. 3 points.
  • N. No newsletter sender had added a fact by 05:30 Helsinki on 23 November. The post went out on a Sunday evening. 0 points.
  • L. OpenAI, Pachocki and Altman posted before any newsletter. 1 point.

The sum is 3P + N + L = 3 + 0 + 1 = 4 of 10.

Stakes 7/10. A first shared limit would work as a standard for the sector's research budgets. It is not 9, because no statute and no supervisor adopts it yet.

Thesis

A race needs a finish line, and the firm that draws the line wins twice. OpenAI has published the only ruler it can read and asked its rivals to be measured by it. A limit in the leader's units turns a capability lead into a standard. A standard costs the leader nothing it was not already spending. It costs a challenger a disclosure that the challenger cannot fake. The political consequence follows. The first binding rule on frontier development will not come from a parliament. It will come from the firm that reaches the number first, and parliaments will ratify it, because a number is easier to legislate than a capability.

Antithesis

The opposite reading is that this is a brake, and a cheap one. A firm that protects a lead does not publish its own loss. The proposer's own caveats destroy the instrument. Chain-of-thought raises capability without moving loss, so a laboratory can cross a capability limit while its number stands still. A laboratory with a higher loss can be the more capable. A metric that cannot rank two models cannot bind them. The binding constraints already exist, and none of them is voluntary. The AI Act presumes systemic risk at a compute threshold. The AI Office can demand documents. The BIS licence regime controls the hardware. A voluntary number that no court, buyer or supervisor can use changes nothing. The accord it builds on has no penalty and no duty to publish.

Forecast

By 30 June 2027 at least one other frontier developer, among Google DeepMind, Anthropic, Meta, xAI, Mistral AI and DeepSeek, will publish a log-perplexity or cross-entropy loss figure for its base models, on a stated evaluation set, in a document it controls. Probability 55 %. A figure in a conference talk, a blog post without a dataset, or a benchmark table does not count. The claim resolves on 30 June 2027.

Political consequences

1-2 years. The instrument is the power of the European AI Office to request information from providers of general-purpose models under the AI Act. The actor is the AI Office. The observable is a published request, a chapter of the code of practice or a model documentation form that names training loss or a perplexity threshold as a disclosed item.

3-4 years. The instrument is a statute or a delegated act that sets a numeric development threshold with a duty to report before training. The actor is the United States Congress, which the accord invites to codify its steps, or the European Commission through a delegated act. The observable is a bill reported out of committee, or a Commission proposal, that names the number and the duty.

Market test: mixed, and the pro-business effect dominates. The instrument is a voluntary standard on a metric that the largest laboratory defines. The actor is OpenAI and the six signatories of the accord. The leader chooses the dataset that fixes the line. A challenger must disclose its position on the leader's terms. Entry at the frontier becomes more costly, so the pro-business effect dominates.

What to watch

First, whether a second laboratory publishes its own loss numbers. Second, whether the next request for information from the AI Office asks for a pretraining figure. Third, whether the board committees created by the accord publish any finding. Fourth, whether a buyer writes a loss figure into a tender. Fifth, whether the 0.68 to 0.70 window produces a named capability. The forecast of 27 October resolves on 31 January 2027.
