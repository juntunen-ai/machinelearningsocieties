18 November 2026 - Machine Learning Societies

The answer replaced the index, and the meter starts on 3 December

Signal 6/10 · Stakes 7/10

Google said on 17 November that Gemini 4 Hydrogen is live in Google Search for all users worldwide, and called it the biggest model upgrade to Search yet. Search now answers, runs several searches at once, and returns tables, images and tools. On 3 December Google must give British publishers the click data that shows what those answers removed.

Signal

Observed. Google published the upgrade on 12 November and repeated the global availability from its own account on 17 November at 22:35 UTC. Four things change. Search reads a multi-part question as one request. It widens “query fan-out”, the technique that runs many searches at once across the subtopics of a question, and Google says this reaches “a broader range of helpful links”. Answers carry more images, comparison tables and small tools. Language coverage widens to every language AI Mode supports. Hydrogen is the fast model of the Gemini 4 family; Carbon and Argon carry longer reasoning. Google gives no number for users, links shown or traffic sent to websites.

The timing needs care: the 12 November post already said Hydrogen was “now available globally in Google Search for all users”. The 17 November message restates the launch.

Three weeks later the measurement falls due. Britain's Competition and Markets Authority imposed a conduct requirement on Google in 2026. Under it Google must give United Kingdom publishers click data by 3 December 2026, meaning the clicks that their appearances in AI features produce. Google's AI opt-out control, open worldwide since 31 August 2026, is bundled: a publisher excludes its content from all AI features or from none. Page-level controls are expected by 3 March 2027.

Caveats. The only causal estimate is a preprint, arXiv:2608.18352, from Pennsylvania and Northeastern. In a preregistered field experiment with 1,100 United States participants in March 2026, people forced into AI Mode for ten days clicked through to outside websites 18.8 percentage points less often, with a 95 % interval of 15.3 to 22.2 points. They visited news sites, Reddit and Wikipedia less, rated Google lower on trust, and moved 11.2 points to rival engines. The paper is not peer reviewed, the sample is young and leans left, and AI Mode carried no advertising then.

An engineering bound limits the other side. Query fan-out multiplies the retrieval work behind one question, so the links an answer carries are set by a latency budget, not by editorial intent. A wider list of links is not more clicks. Google's counter-claim, billions of clicks a week, is a volume without a denominator.

Assessment. The unit of public information moved from a ranked list to a generated answer, and the ranked list carried a payment: the visit, and the advertising behind it. The new arrangement keeps the answer inside Google and makes the visit an option Google grants. The remedy in force is disclosure, not payment. Stakes are seven because a regulator attached a measuring duty to a product that now reaches every market, and because the rule on who is paid for being read is being rewritten by a conduct remedy, not a statute.

Evidence 6/10.

  • P = 1: two posts by Google, the party that shipped the change, and the regulator's conduct requirement.
  • N = 2: two newsletter senders added a fact of their own, one on citation tracking, one on the click-data deadline.
  • L = 1: Google published first, on 12 November, and restated it on 17 November before the newsletters.

The sum is 3 × 1 + 2 + 1 = 6.

Stakes 7/10. A disclosure duty now attaches to the referral rule of a whole publishing sector, while the statute that would set payment is unwritten.

Thesis

Search has turned from an index that sends people away into an answer that keeps them. The referral is now a decision Google makes, not a property a publisher holds. Regulation has seen the decision and asked for the numbers, which is weaker than a price.

Antithesis

Google may be right that the total is stable. The one causal study forced people into a mode they had not chosen, on a sample that does not represent the public; a preference test is not a market measurement. Fan-out surfaces small sites that never ranked, so fewer clicks per impression can sit beside more publishers reached. The British remedy is national, and Google extended it to the world in August, which suggests the market moved faster than a statute would. And for most questions an answer with a table and three sources is a better product than ten blue links; a levy would tax the improvement.

Forecast

Horizon: to 30 June 2027. Probability: 55 %. By that date the Competition and Markets Authority, the European Commission or a national competition authority will open a formal proceeding, or accept a binding commitment, that treats generative answers in general search as a separate market or an anti-competitive tie, or that requires referral metrics for each answer. A public document that names generative answers that way resolves the claim as a hit; otherwise it is a miss. The wording stays frozen.

Political consequences

1-2 years. The instrument is the conduct requirement, with click data due on 3 December 2026 and page-level controls on 3 March 2027, plus the AI Act transparency duty that Traficom supervises in Finland. The actors are the authority and Traficom. The observable is published click data, a page-level control, or an enforcement letter that names AI answers. Evidence tier: Observed for the deadlines, Assessment for the effect. Market test: pro-business; the instrument is the bundled opt-out and the missing referral duty, the actor is Google, and Google gains, because a publisher that cannot separate the features cannot price the access.

3-4 years. The instrument is a payment right for generative answers, either an extension of the press publishers' right in Article 15 of the DSM Directive or a national licensing statute. The actors are the European Parliament and the Council, or a national legislature. The observable is a text that names generative answers, fixes who pays and sets a rate. Evidence tier: Assessment.

What to watch

  • Google's click data for United Kingdom publishers on 3 December 2026, and whether it goes out worldwide.
  • Whether Reddit renews its data licence with Google, after reports on 18 November that a USD 60 million deal may end.
  • Whether page-level controls arrive by 3 March 2027, which would let a publisher price each feature.
  • Any Traficom or AI Office decision that names a generated answer as content needing a label.
How the Signal is scored

Every edition prints two numbers, read together. The first measures the quality of the evidence. The second measures how much changes if the evidence holds.

Signal, 0–10: the evidence. The formula is E = 3P + N + L, and the maximum is ten.

  • P — primary source. Three points when the claim rests on the actor's own document, announcement or statement. Zero when it rests on reporting about the actor.
  • N — independent confirmation. One point for each newsletter or newsroom that adds a fact of its own. Repeating a press release adds nothing.
  • L — first publisher. One point when the party at the centre of the story published it first.

Stakes, 1–10: the consequence. 1 is one team's experiment. 3 is one workflow in one organisation. 5 is a workflow adopted across a sector. 7 is a procurement rule or standard that changes. 9 is a statute, a liability rule or a supervision rule. 10 is a change in market structure. The even steps are the cases between them.
