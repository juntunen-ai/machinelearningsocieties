# Machine Learning Societies — standing background

The brief tracks technological diffusion: how a technology actually spreads through politics, the economy, work and law, and who ends up using it. Following David Edgerton, it covers technologies-in-use rather than technologies-in-the-making. One signal, one thesis, one falsifiable dated forecast.

## Sources

**Stewart Brand, "Pace Layering: How Complex Systems Learn and Keep Learning"**
Journal of Design and Science, MIT Press, 18 January 2018, DOI 10.21428/7f2e5f08.
https://jods.mitpress.mit.edu/pub/issue3-brand/release/2

Six layers of different pace and size, fast to slow: fashion and art, commerce, infrastructure, governance, culture, nature. Fast layers innovate and absorb shocks; slow layers stabilise and remember. Use it as the timescale test: ask which layer a signal moved, and which slower layer must absorb it before the change counts as diffusion. A movement confined to the fast layer is an event, not adoption. Contradiction between layers is normal, and it is where the friction in an edition usually sits.

**normaltech.ai** — framing: AI as normal technology, a story of diffusion, adoption and politics rather than a single superintelligence event.

**ai-2040.com** — counter-position, used as a stress test for an edition's thesis, never as the brief's own forecast.

**Acemoglu and the Humanist Review** — on diffusion, productivity and who captures the gains.

**AI diffusion job watch** — daily scan of 27 AI-native vendors' job boards, about 157 tracked deployment requisitions, curated to one candidate profile. A supply-side indicator of the deployment layer only; not a census of AI hiring and not evidence of enterprise demand.

## Rules

- A signal may sit at one level only: political, social, institutional, organisational or individual. A thesis claiming all five at once needs evidence from more than one.
- Background sources shape story selection, caveats and framing. They are not edition subjects and are never written up as the signal itself.
- Pace layering is a diagnostic, not decoration: name the layer in the assessment when it decides whether the change holds.
