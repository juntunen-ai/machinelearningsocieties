Diffusion Index v0.6: Digital, Physical, and Institutional Pace-Layer Measurement

The Machine Learning Societies Diffusion Index (v0.6) measures how artificial intelligence moves from frontier model announcements into enterprise production, physical hardware, regulatory standards, electrical grids, and planetary boundaries. It is built in the open according to the OECD/JRC Handbook on Constructing Composite Indicators (2008).

§0 · Verification Discipline: [V] Verified vs. [K] Candidate Sources

Every data source in index/registry.v0.6.json is tagged with an explicit verification flag:

  • [V] Verified ("verified": true — 27 sources): Primary file, API endpoint, or statutory filing has been directly opened and verified against its primary release.
  • [K] Candidate ("verified": false — 22 sources): Plausible from prior institutional knowledge or secondary reporting, but primary table or licence terms await direct inspection.

Hard compiler rule: Nothing marked [K] may enter index/series.csv until verified. Our build script (scripts/fetch_open_series.py and scripts/compute_index.py) fails with an error if any observation in series.csv references an unverified source.

§1 · Three-Block Separation & Digital vs. Physical Channels

A composite index that mixes inputs, adoption, constraints, and consequences into a single number cannot be used to test whether conditions accelerate diffusion or whether diffusion causes consequences. Methodology v0.6 separates the ten pillars into three distinct blocks:

  • Block A — Diffusion Core (Headline Index): 1. Deployment, 2. Cost to Serve, and 3. Capital Intensity. Only Block A enters the headline Diffusion Index.
  • Block B — Enabling Conditions: 5. Governance Burden, 6. Human Capital, 8. Energy & Grid Capacity, and 10. Market Openness.
  • Block C — Societal & Planetary Consequences: 4. Labour Reorganisation, 7. Ecological Balance, and 9. Ownership Distribution.

Within Block A, every pillar is decomposed into two channels: the Digital AI Index (DAI) (software, cloud APIs, enterprise workflows) and the Physical AI & Robotics Index (PAI) (industrial robots, cleared clinical medical devices, autonomous vehicles, grid hardware). Because IFR industrial robot counts (542,076 installed in 2024; operational stock 4,663,698) include non-AI industrial automation, PAI carries an explicit ai_specificity = proxy badge.

§2 · Stewart Brand Pace Layers & Daily Brief Signal Ingestion

Following Stewart Brand's Pace Layering (2018), societies change at six different speeds, from fast to slow: Fashion/Attention → Commerce → Infrastructure → Governance → Culture → Nature. Fast layers innovate and propose; slow layers integrate, constrain, and stabilise.

  • Fashion / Attention (viral demos, benchmark press releases) is deliberately not scored in the index.
  • Commerce (P1 Deployment, P2 Cost to Serve, P3 Capital Intensity) moves in quarters.
  • Infrastructure (P8 Energy & Grid Capacity) moves in years to decades.
  • Governance (P5 Governance Burden, P9 Ownership Distribution, P10 Market Openness) moves in years.
  • Culture & Learning (P4 Labour Reorganisation, P6 Human Capital) moves in years to generations.
  • Nature (P7 Ecological Balance & Planetary Boundaries) moves in decades to centuries.

How Daily Briefs feed the Index (§2.1): Every daily brief published in briefs/YYYY-MM-DD.html is parsed by scripts/sync_briefs_to_index.py into index/brief_signals.csv. Each brief is mapped to its primary Pillar, NACE Sector, Block (A/B/C), Channel, Layer (Reach / Depth / Embeddedness), and Stewart Brand Pace Layer. When a daily brief verifies a Tier 1–2 primary quantitative datapoint — such as Anthropic PBC's SEC Form S-1 ($16.29B H1 revenue, $8.95B compute cost, $265.6B purchase commitments, 48% cloud marketplace concentration in the 28 Oct 2026 brief), Texas TCEQ/ERCOT gating 8.7 GW of data-centre gas permits (25 Oct 2026 brief), or the citation of harmonised standard EN 18286:2026 in the EU Official Journal (14 Oct 2026 brief) — that verified figure is logged directly into index/series.csv with a brief_ref date pointer.

§3 · The Ten Pillars, Polarity, and the J-Curve Complement

  • Pillar 1 — Deployment (direction: +1): Anchored by Eurostat isoc_eb_ai (EU-27 20.0% in 2025, +6.5 pp/yr; Denmark 42.0%, Finland 37.8%, Romania 5.2%), Statistics Finland table 13vg (38% all firms, 68% large firms, ~80% ICT), US Census BTOS (with explicit break_flag on 17 Nov 2025), Eurostat individual gen-AI use (32.7%), IFR robotics (proxy), and FDA AI/ML medical devices (1,620+).
  • Pillar 2 — Cost to Serve (direction: -1): Tracks task execution cost including oversight and integration. Single-shot token cost claims (e.g., GDPval's headline 100× claim) are never logged as a cell because they omit multi-step workflow failure (Stanford HAI hospital benchmark: <50% end-to-end completion).
  • Pillar 3 — Capital Intensity & J-Curve Intangible Multiplier (direction: +1): Audited compute and capex commitments ($265.6B in Anthropic's S-1) are recorded as a lower bound alongside Brynjolfsson, Rock & Syverson's (2021) 2×–10× sensitivity band for unmeasured organisational and process complements.
  • Pillars 4, 5, 7, 9 (direction: 0 — Diagnostic): Labour Reorganisation, Governance Burden, Ecological Balance, and Ownership Distribution are reported as diagnostic levels without normative polarity.

§4 · 10 × 10 Sector Grid, Comparable Sector Gap & Three Layers

Every Pillar × NACE Rev. 2.1 Sector cell separates three diffusion layers: Reach (any use), Depth (share of workflows/tasks), and Embeddedness (process, procurement line-item, or audit-trail integration). The Comparable Sector Gap is computed strictly between sectors of Grade A or B provenance (e.g., Information & Software at 69.0 vs. Agriculture & Forestry at 18.1, or Finland ICT ~80% vs. all enterprises 38%).

§5 · Five-Tier Source Hierarchy & Series Breaks

Sources are graded by provenance (A official register/filing, B academic/intergovernmental/audited series, C proxy/single-source) and frequency (M monthly, Q quarterly, Y annual) across five trust tiers: Tier 1 (Official statistics), Tier 2 (Regulator/intergovernmental registers), Tier 3 (Academic/open non-profit), Tier 4 (Vendor telemetry/company disclosures — never sole source for a Grade A/B cell), and Tier 5 (Secondary aggregators such as Stanford AI Index — used only to locate primaries, never logged as a cell). Questionnaire changes (such as the US Census BTOS 17 Nov 2025 expansion or Eurostat 2025 scope updates) carry break_flag = true and an explicit bridge_factor.

§7 · Fixed-Goalpost Scoring & 1,000-Draw Monte Carlo Uncertainty

For each indicator \(i\) with fixed physiological/institutional floor \(L_i\) and ceiling \(U_i\), the level score on \([0, 100]\) is:

\[ S_i(t) = \operatorname{clamp}_{[0,100]}\!\left(100 \cdot \frac{g(x_i(t)) - g(L_i)}{g(U_i) - g(L_i)}\right) \]

where \(g(x) = x\) for bounded percentage shares and \(g(x) = \ln(x)\) for skewed physical quantities (GW, TWh, USD billions, robot density). Every Block A, DAI, and PAI release runs 1,000 Monte Carlo draws in scripts/compute_index.py varying pillar weights \(\mathbf{w} \sim \operatorname{Dirichlet}(\mathbf{1})\), indicator noise \(\epsilon_i \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_i^2)\), and arithmetic vs. geometric aggregation, publishing the median and 5th–95th percentile band \([p_5, p_{95}]\).

Diffuusioindeksi v0.6: digitaalisen, fyysisen ja institutionaalisen kerrostuman mittaus

Machine Learning Societies -julkaisun Diffuusioindeksi (v0.6) mittaa, miten tekoäly siirtyy huippumallien julkistuksista yritysten tuotantoon, fyysisiin laitteisiin, viranomaisstandardeihin, sähköverkkoihin ja planetaarisiin rajoihin. Indeksi rakennetaan avoimesti OECD:n ja JRC:n Handbook on Constructing Composite Indicators (2008) -ohjeistuksen mukaisesti.

§0 · Vahvistuskuri: [V] vahvistetut vs. [K] ehdokaslähteet

Jokainen tiedostossa index/registry.v0.6.json oleva tietolähde on merkitty eksplisiittisellä vahvistustunnisteella:

  • [V] Vahvistettu ("verified": true — 27 lähdettä): Alkuperäistiedosto, avoin API-rajapinta tai viranomaisrekisteri on avattu ja tarkistettu suoraan alkuperäislähteestä.
  • [K] Ehdokaslähde ("verified": false — 22 lähdettä): Tunnettu institutionaalinen lähde, jonka taulukkorakenne tai lisenssiehdot odottavat vielä suoraa tarkistusta.

Ehdoton sääntö: Yhtäkään [K]-merkittyä lähdettä ei saa kirjata index/series.csv-aikasarjatauluun ennen vahvistusta. Kääntäjäskripti keskeyttää ajon virheeseen, jos yksikin havainto viittaa vahvistamattomaan lähteeseen.

§1 · Kolmilohkoinen erottelu sekä digitaalinen vs. fyysinen kanava

Indeksi, joka sekoittaa panokset, käyttöönoton, rajoitteet ja seuraukset yhdeksi luvuksi, ei pysty testaamaan syy-seuraussuhteita. Metodologia v0.6 erottaa kymmenen pilaria kolmeen lohkoon:

  • Lohko A — Diffuusioydin (Pääindeksi): 1. Käyttöönotto, 2. Suorituskustannus ja 3. Pääomavaltaisuus. Vain Lohko A muodostaa varsinaisen päädiffuusioindeksin.
  • Lohko B — Mahdollistavat edellytykset: 5. Sääntely- ja valvontakuorma, 6. Inhimillinen pääoma, 8. Energia- ja verkkokapasiteetti sekä 10. Markkinoiden avoimuus.
  • Lohko C — Yhteiskunnalliset ja planetaariset seuraukset: 4. Työn uudelleenjärjestely, 7. Ekologinen tasapaino ja 9. Omistuksen jakautuminen.

Lohkon A sisällä jokainen pilari jaetaan kahteen kanavaan: Digitaalinen tekoälyindeksi (DAI) ja Fyysinen tekoäly- ja robotiikkaindeksi (PAI). Koska IFR:n teollisuusrobottiluvut (542 076 asennettua robottia vuonna 2024; kanta 4 663 698) sisältävät myös muun kuin tekoälyohjatun automaation, PAI kantaa nimenomaista ai_specificity = proxy -varoitusta.

§2 · Stewart Brandin vauhtikerrokset ja päiväkatsausten signaaliputki

Stewart Brandin Pace Layering (2018) -mallin mukaisesti yhteiskunnat muuttuvat kuudella eri nopeudella nopeimmasta hitaimpaan: Muoti/Huomio → Kauppa → Infrastruktuuri → Hallinta/Sääntely → Kulttuuri → Luonto.

Miten päiväkatsaukset syöttävät indeksiä (§2.1): Skripti scripts/sync_briefs_to_index.py jäsentää jokaisen julkaistun päiväkatsauksen (briefs/YYYY-MM-DD.html) tiedostoon index/brief_signals.csv ja liittää vahvistetut tason 1–2 luvut (kuten Anthropicin S-1 -listalleottoasiakirjan luvut 28.10.2026 katsauksesta, Texasin 8,7 GW:n lupajonon 25.10.2026 katsauksesta ja EN 18286:2026 -standardin 14.10.2026 katsauksesta) suoraan index/series.csv-tauluun brief_ref-viitteellä.

§7 · Kiinteät maalitolpat ja 1 000 vedon Monte Carlo -epävarmuuslaskenta

Jokainen indikaattori pisteytetään kiinteiden ala- ja ylärajojen \([L_i, U_i]\) avulla asteikolle \([0, 100]\). Skripti scripts/compute_index.py ajaa jokaiselle julkaisulle 1 000 Monte Carlo -vetoa (Dirichlet-painot, indikaattorikohina sekä aritmeettinen vs. geometrinen keskiarvo) ja julkaisee mediaanin sekä 90 % luottamusvälin \([p_5, p_{95}]\).
